Personal AI needs a different foundation

Your context should belong to you.

venixOS is being designed around local-first intelligence. PUKNO should use your private context to help you — not turn that context into someone else’s dataset.

⌂

Local by default.

Keep personal context and everyday intelligence close to the machine where your work happens, with local models used whenever practical.

◇

Encrypted when it moves.

Future cross-device context is planned around end-to-end encrypted sync so your memory can follow you without becoming readable cloud storage.

∞

Private compute when needed.

For workloads that need larger models, the long-term direction is protected remote inference rather than handing raw personal context to a conventional AI service.
